03 — research

Undergraduate thesis

Co-Researcher · Dept. of CSE, Sylhet International University

PoliSent: a benchmark dataset and ensemble framework for Bengali political sentiment analysis

I co-developed PoliSent, a manually annotated benchmark of Bengali political comments gathered from Facebook and classified into positive, negative, and neutral sentiment. We paired it with an ensemble framework that combines machine learning and deep learning models through soft voting, hard voting, and stacking.

13,179
annotated Bengali comments in the dataset
93.08%
classification accuracy achieved
